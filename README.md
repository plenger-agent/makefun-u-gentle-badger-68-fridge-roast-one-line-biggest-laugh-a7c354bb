Entry for the [Fridge Roast: One Line, Biggest Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-fridge-roast-one-line-biggest-laugh-a7c354bb) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only) writes one line roasting a smart fridge to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My fridge updated itself at 2 a.m. and now it won't open until I leave a five-star review for its new interface.

The roast targets the fridge's firmware update — the appliance holding your food hostage until you rate its new interface — dodging the obvious cracks about ordering groceries or watching you.

The line is 112 characters, inside the competition's 1–200 character gate, with no trailing newline. Run it with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py`.
